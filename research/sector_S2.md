## 6. Sector dynamics
- **Consumer health:** growth decelerated in 2025-26. HLN went from 9% organic (2022) to about 2.5% guided for 2026. PRGO is negative. The causes are price normalisation, weak US cold and flu seasons, retailer destocking and private-label substitution. Consolidation is happening: KMB/KVUE.
- **Chronic-care consumables (COLO, CTEC)** grow a steady 5-7%. The main risk is **US reimbursement**:
  - CMS DMEPOS competitive bidding for ostomy and continence. CTEC estimates a 1-2% hit to group sales, no earlier than 2028.
  - Skin-substitute payment reform cut CTEC's InnovaMatrix by more than 90% and pushed COLO's Biologics (Kerecis) into decline.
- **Vision/hearing/dental** are driven by demographics and procedures, and are partly discretionary:
  - Galderma, Straumann, Demant and EL are at the top of their growth ranges in 2026.
  - CooperVision has stalled.
  - China VBP is a pricing risk for dental implants (STMN; the round was delayed in 2026) and for orthopaedics and medtech (SN, BDX).
- **Diagnostics (BIM):** respiratory seasonality causes volatility. Non-respiratory syndromic panels are growing by more than 20%.
- **Tariffs:** IEEPA tariff refunds flattered Q3/Q4-2026 results at STE and BDX.

## 7. Measurable scale economics
- **STMN:** core EBIT margin of about 26% versus low-teens at Dentsply and Envista, with about a third of the global implant market.
- **COLO:** EBIT margin of 25-28% versus about 23% at CTEC in overlapping ostomy and continence. Coloplast's margin is now falling.
- **EL:** both the lens and the frame businesses are larger than the next competitor.
- **ALC:** largest installed base of ophthalmic surgical equipment, which pulls through consumables.
- **STE:** density of the AST network. Validated sterilisation capacity is hard to replicate.
- **HLN vs KVUE:** HLN's adjusted operating margin is 22.9%. Its scale advantage over peers is modest.

## 9. Contrary evidence
- Several "defensive" names cut guidance in Q3/Q4-2026: HLN, PRGO, SN and COO.
- Consumer health is less defensive than assumed. Volume growth is near zero at HLN (+0.7% FY25).
- Chronic-care consumables carry concentrated US reimbursement policy risk.
- EL's wearables growth is tied to the Meta partnership, an AI-consumer theme. It is about 6.5% of Q3 revenue.
