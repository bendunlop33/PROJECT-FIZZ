# 0017: Guide motion, round 2 — continuous turns, knee drive, frame cadence

**Status:** Proposed, 2026-10-10 (developer, for review). Built and tested on Linux; **not yet seen on an iPhone**. Supersedes ADR 0015 only where stated (turning pass keys; J4 alone is not enough). The plan is the round-2 motion plan (S1–S8); this ADR records what is built so far and what is still open.

## Context
After ADR 0015, Ben's "the joints don't operate smoothly in one seamless motion" still had measurable causes: an acceleration step where a channel turns back at a pass key (overhead-press pelvis 69% of its phase peak, split-squat root.x 28–29%), the squat knee snapping straight in the last frames (the hip's minimum-jerk slow-down magnified by IK near a straight knee: M8 44%, J5 3.67×), and app playback holding some frames for 1–3 refreshes (rounding put every frame change exactly on a vsync; the bench's 2550 ms is 76.5 frames).

## Decision

### E5: acceleration stays continuous through a turning pass key (built)
In phase mode a channel that turns (or goes flat) at a pass key keeps slope 0 there, and now keeps acceleration continuous too: `phaseTurnSlopes` (math.ts) is the minimum-curvature cubic with the fixed slopes, C2 at every interior knot the monotone guard leaves room for (a clipped knot is reported as a kink). Projection channels are excluded (a rounder projection turn pulls the overhead-press shoulder into a 5.9° reversal). Phases without a stopped pass key keep `splineSlopes`, bit for bit. **Supersedes** ADR 0015's "turning keys decouple both sides".

### E6: knee drive, opt-in (built; no guide uses it yet)
`"drive": { "joint": "kneeNear", "solves": "root.y" }` (schemaVersion stays 1) for `motion: "phases"` side views whose near foot is planted on the floor at every key. Per phase the near-knee flexion at each key is a C2 spline in the phase's minimum-jerk progress (E5 rules); the hip height is solved in closed form so the leg's IK gives that flexion (`drive.ts`; `pose.ts` `drivenStateAt`). root.x and the pelvis keep their own splines. Key poses are bit-identical, stops keep zero velocity and acceleration, planted feet don't move. Metrics and checks sample `drivenStateAt`/`poseAt`, never bare `stateAt`.
- New hard checks: **D1** (guide:motion) a pass key's flexion within 0.5° of its phase's stop-to-stop line in u, the best measured shape; `--suggest` prints the pass-key root.y that puts it there. **drive-miss** (guide:motion D2 and the guide:check `drive` item): no frame without a solution.
- On the committed squat with drive on and the pass keys on the line: M7v 1.57×, M8 12%, J5 1.07×, J3 97 ms / 13.6%, but the bar starts late and finishes early (bar M3 4%). The squat needs re-authoring with the plan's solver (S4, guide-maker). **If no squat passes every gate, revert E6** (no unused format field) and take the plan's option B (a softer standing knee).

### E7: leg drive (built 10 Oct 2026; the squat uses it)
`"drive": { "joint": "legNear", "solves": "root.xy" }`, same preconditions as E6. The near **shin angle** (ankle → knee, from vertical) and the **knee flexion** are each a C2 spline in the phase's minimum-jerk progress through their key values; the hip is their forward kinematics up from the planted ankle (`hipFromLeg`). Root x/y are therefore no longer splines of their own for this guide; the pelvis, spine, neck and arms keep theirs.
- **Why:** with the hip as two Cartesian channels, the shin angle was an uncontrolled by-product of root x and y. On the committed squat the knee point stopped dead mid-descent (0.00 H/s at t .44 while the hips moved at 0.24) and mid-ascent, the ankle angle reversed 0.5°, and the shin ran 121 ms ahead of the knee (J3 fail). Re-tuning one key's root couldn't fix it (a ±0.01/±0.015 sweep never gave a one-hump, non-reversing shin). With the leg drive each joint is one monotone Hermite per segment, so it can't stall or turn back between keys.
- **Pass keys** carry a shared progress for shin and knee, front-loaded on the way down (so the bar starts down promptly: with near-straight knees the bar drop goes as the square of the joint angles) and solved separately on the way up (mirroring the descent left the bar and hips with a speed shoulder near lockout). The pelvis at each pass key keeps the bar within its drift limit.
- **D1** for a leg-drive guide reports the shin against the knee's share of its travel (`in step`), for reference; pass/fail sync is J3's job (ankle–knee pair). `--suggest` prints the hip the key's shin and knee give.
- **Squat result** (n 1200): ankle reversal 0.52° → 0, knee-point mid-phase minimum 0% → 38% of peak, ankle–knee t50 121 → 0 ms, every guide:motion gate passes (J6 far-knee reach is the pre-existing warning). The bar now moves up to 0.026 H horizontally (limit 0.03): about 2.5 cm back early in the descent and forward again near lockout.
- **Considered and dropped:** solving the pelvis per frame so the bar stays exactly over mid-foot (`barLine`). It works (drift 0.00003 H, bar one clean bell), but with the bar locked the bar can only start down through knee flexion, so its speed at τ 0.2 is 4–9% of peak (M3 fails), and front-loading the joints enough to fix that puts a second hump in the hip and pelvis. Not built into the format (no unused field). **Ben's call:** a bar that stays exactly vertical but starts and finishes slowly, or the current 2.5 cm wander with a prompt start.

### Round 3 pose and drawing fixes (built 10 Oct 2026; review of the same day)
- **Sled push:** pre-swing keys at t .365 and .865 (the trailing foot's sole 35° there), then the heel rises faster into toe-off (55° at lift-off, was 50°) and keeps rising into the swing (66° at the Switch key, was 35°; that key's foot 0.045 H further back and 0.015 H lower), so the knee stops extending before lift-off: 5.4° → 1.9° of extension after toe-off. M7v 3.98× → 2.14×, M8 38.9% → 21%. **J5 is still 3.0×** (limit 2×, a gait warning): the lift-off joins stance (linear) and swing (cubic) with continuous velocity but not acceleration; a C2 contact needs a format change, not a pose.
- **Bench press:** the bar touches the lower chest (x −0.118 → −0.08, 0.088 H footward of the shoulder joint), the forearm is vertical, and the elbow ends at the pad top (arm projection 0.615, forearm 0.745); `barDriftMax` 0.08 → 0.09 for the over-the-shoulders-to-lower-chest J path; the arms are no longer exempt from the bench collider, so `no-penetration` holds the elbow above the pad.
- **One-arm row:** Top shoulder −22° → −10° (elbow 100°), so the upper arm sits inside the torso outline instead of doubling the back line; Halfway re-solved on the joint line (J3 0 ms). The start dumbbell still overlaps the bench pad: it hangs on the near side and the bench is on the far side, so the overlap is real; clearing it would need the dumbbell 0.11 H higher, and drawing the pad in front would be wrong. Left for Ben.
- **Leg press:** Bottom knee 95° → 85°, thigh–trunk angle 37° → 53° (seat back 40° → 30°, pelvis −50° → −60°, plate travel 0 → 0.03; Start 0.154 → 0.15 so the knee is a little softer at the top). The hip and pelvis are constant over the cycle (tested). P1 now warns (0.017): its chord heuristic pulls Halfway to 89% of the travel; kept at the min-jerk line.
- **Drawing:** thin body-shade arm edge at the S tier instead of a background halo; open arm strokes fade over their last 0.05 H; no iliac-crest waist line; kneecap line fades out at 55–70° of knee flexion; hip fillet in side views; side-view faint pelvis shapes go under the near leg's colour; forearm/grip tints only from 240 pt and no soft region under 6 pt²; triceps cover the back half of the upper arm, front delts −0.12–0.45 d0.9. New `guide:check` item `target-legible` (strong colour ≥ 10 pt² at 115 pt on the best labelled key). Details in exercise-guides-v2 §3.6, §3.8, §3.11, §4.3.
- **Not changed:** the lat pulldown's elbow-leads-shoulder allowance (J3 283 ms) is geometric (front view, vertical bar, fixed grip): dropping or re-timing the pass key moves it only to 272–290 ms. It waits for Ben's pose choice.

### Metrics (built; guide:motion, in `npm run check` since 10 Oct 2026)
M7v (velocity-vector frame step ≤ 2.5× the minimum-jerk ideal), M8 (the same step near stops ≤ 25% of peak; catches the lockout snap J4 misses), J5 (IK knee/elbow angular-acceleration step ≤ 2×), J6 (reach of a moving undriven IK limb ≤ 0.99, warning), C6 (seam velocity ≤ 0.02 H/s, hard, also in guide:check). Gait guides report M7v, M8 and J5 as warnings. Per-guide declared excursions (overhead-press pelvis lean-back, 6°) and allowances live in `MOTION_CONFIG` with their reason and are printed in every report; hard limits can't be relaxed.

### V5: playback cadence (built)
- `frameIndexAt(elapsed, duration, count, loop, dtMs)` samples half a refresh ahead (dt capped at 50 ms) and rounds down, so every frame change falls half a refresh from a vsync. A ping-pong shows 0…n−1 forwards and n…1 back, one interval each. The player passes `timeSincePreviousFrame`.
- `guide:check` **duration-grid** (hard): `durationMs × 30 / 1000` is whole. The bench goes 2550 → 2600 ms (2% slower; key t values are normalised, poses unchanged). Tempo note for Ben.
- A seeded cadence simulation (every guide duration, 60/120 Hz, 0–200 µs jitter) finds zero off-cadence frames; the old clock fails the same simulation.

## Consequences
- Guides with no turning pass key and no `drive` sample exactly as before; key poses are unchanged everywhere. `guide:motion -- --all`: 10/10 pass since round 3 (warnings: J6 reach on the squat, bench, press and RDL; sled J5 3.0×; leg-press P1). Round 3 is built and measured on Linux only; it still needs a look on Ben's iPhone.
- Playback holds every baked frame for the same number of refreshes on a steady display; confirming it needs a slow-motion screen recording on the phone.
