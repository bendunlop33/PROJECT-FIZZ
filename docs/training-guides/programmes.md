# Programme comparison

*Training-researcher survey for Ben. All sources checked 2026-10-08. Not medical advice. Summaries only. Paid books aren't reproduced; where only a paid version exists, Ben can add his own copy to the app library.*

**Summary**
1. Best fit for Ben (strength + endurance/HYROX, limited time, uses RPE): a **5/3/1 two- or three-day template with modest supplemental work**, or a **4-day upper/lower with RPE-based top set + back-offs**, combined with an endurance plan. Both leave room for running/riding.
2. Beginner linear programmes (Starting Strength, StrongLifts, GZCLP, Reddit PPL) are well-documented and free, but too frequent and too progression-aggressive for a trained concurrent athlete. Their stall/reset rules are still useful fallback logic.
3. RP Hypertrophy and Juggernaut are good models for the app's coach (feedback-driven volume; AMAP-driven training-max updates). They're paid and built for single-goal lifters.
4. HYROX-specific plans are almost all paid (The Progrm, HWPO, HYROX GOAL Hybrid Program, Repz, Athletica). None publishes a free adult plan. Use them as structure references only.
5. Every programme's numbers must still pass the app's guardrails: achievable loads, equipment max, pain stops progression, future changes are proposals.

## Fit notes for Ben (inference)

- **Time-limited, concurrent:** prefer programmes with low main-lift volume and few lifting days (5/3/1 2–3 day, upper/lower 3–4 day). Volume-ramping hypertrophy blocks belong in off-season phases with reduced running.
- **RPE user:** top set + back-off and RPE-based templates use the data Ben already logs, and match the coach's adjust-on-signal design (ADR 0006).
- **Guardrail conflicts:**
  - Linear "add weight every session" rules can propose increments the gym can't achieve (e.g. +2.5 kg on dumbbells with 2 kg steps per hand). The domain layer's snapping/ceiling logic must win.
  - Percentage tables from books produce non-achievable loads. Always round via `roundToAvailableLoad`.
  - AMRAP/"+" sets are effectively RPE 9–10 by design. The ADR 0006 trigger should not fire an "adjust?" offer on a planned AMRAP (see evidence-core flag).
  - No programme's pain or injury advice is imported. Rule 9 governs.
