One strip, two views

  • Block | Overall sits under Lifts as a plain text strip, not a second pill bar.
  • Block (default while a plan runs): your main lifts this block, week by week, with what changed or Stalled. No period bar; the weeks are the period. Tap a lift for its block detail. Blocks › for past ones.
  • Overall: exactly what Lifts shows today, the period bar, chips, chart, table and records, without the block section on top.
  • No plan running: no strip, just Overall.
  • Training and Body keep their period bar.

Not changing

Bottom tabs stay Today · Library · Progress · Profile. The calendar stays in Training › Month.
