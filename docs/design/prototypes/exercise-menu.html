My recommendation: A, with C as a shortcut. A is the native iOS menu, so it looks premium for free and everyone already knows how it behaves, and it's the least work to keep looking good. C makes the three most common actions one swipe. B is neat, but it takes space on a screen you use between sets. Notes, Plates and Adjust stay in the menu when they apply; Undo appears at the top of the menu when there's something to undo.
