Your plan, at a glance

Tap the plan in Progress and you get this instead of the setup questions. Edit (top right, or the button at the bottom) opens Gym goals.

What's on it

  • Title: focus · length. Line under it: the week you're in and the end date.
  • Week strip: finished weeks are filled and this week is bright. The deload week is dashed with a ↓.
  • Three numbers: days a week, typical minutes, effort.
  • Days: each day's main lift and how many exercises. Green dot = done this week, Next = what Today starts. Tap a day to open it.
  • Main lifts: where you are now and the week 6 estimate, marked est.
  • Sets per week: the same ranges as Plan › Muscles; amber = below range. Tap through to Rebalance.

Left off on purpose

  • No explanations of the rules and no ticks. A "Why?" only appears where you can already tap for one.
  • No priorities or likes and dislikes. Those stay in Edit.
